Piano Major Clock
By James Bowden
January 2005

This clock, unlike most others for the Steve's Clock program, does not actually speak as such.  Instead, a piano plays.

Each number and announcement has a different musical chord or phrase.  For those who want to know: this clock plays in the key of D major and most chords are based around the scale of D.

It helps if you have perfect or relative pitch, but if you want to understand this clock at all, you will need to identify intervals in a major scale.

But hey, don't worry if you can't actually interpret this clock, I hope you enjoy the music it produces.

One little word of advice: this clock works best in twelve hour style; if you use 24 hour format, some times may start or end on an interesting cliff-hanger.

Watch out for Piano Minor clock.
